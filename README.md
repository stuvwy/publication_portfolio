# Publication Portfolio

Publication portfolio for tracking milestones, deadlines, effort, budget,
priorities, bibliographic status, and institutional recognition across
planned and ongoing publications.

The primary purpose is to prioritize limited PI effort across competing
publication opportunities while ensuring that completed publications are
followed through to bibliographic and institutional completion.

## Portfolio Rule

New publication activities are prioritized by:

- strategic value,
- urgency,
- probability of completion,
- remaining PI effort.

Normally, no more than three manuscripts should be in the active writing
stage simultaneously.

Completed scientific work remains in the active portfolio as long as
bibliographic publication or relevant institutional recognition is still
pending.

## Publication Lifecycle

A generic publication may pass through the following stages:

`Idea → Planned → Concept → Writing → Internal Review → Submitted → Review → Revision → Accepted → Published → Institutionally Recognized → Closed`

Not every publication uses every stage. For example, books, invited
contributions, and conference papers may follow different editorial
workflows.

`Closed` means that no relevant scientific, bibliographic, financial, or
institutional follow-up remains.

## Rating Scales

### Strategic Value

Indicates the overall strategic importance of the publication.

| Score | Meaning |
|---:|---|
| 1 | Low — limited relevance beyond the individual publication |
| 2 | Moderate — useful contribution, but limited strategic impact |
| 3 | Relevant — supports an established research or teaching activity |
| 4 | High — important for research profile, collaboration, or institutional goals |
| 5 | Very high — directly supports key strategic research, publication, or career objectives |

`Strategic Value` should normally remain relatively stable and only
change when the strategic context changes.

### PI Attention

Indicates how strongly the publication currently requires the Principal
Investigator's (PI) own attention.

| Score | Meaning |
|---:|---|
| 1 | No action currently required |
| 2 | Monitor — minor follow-up or administrative action |
| 3 | Prepare — foreseeable PI work, but not immediately critical |
| 4 | Active — substantial PI contribution currently required |
| 5 | Immediate — PI action is currently critical due to deadline, dependency, or opportunity |

`PI Attention` is dynamic and should be updated whenever deadlines,
dependencies, reviews, or publication status change.

### Own Effort

Estimated remaining PI effort:

| Rating | Estimated remaining effort |
|---|---:|
| Very Low | < 2 h |
| Low | 2–8 h |
| Medium | 8–20 h |
| High | 20–40 h |
| Very High | > 40 h |
| TBD | Not yet estimated |

The estimate refers to **remaining PI effort**, not the overall effort of
all authors.

## Deadlines and Triggers

The `Next Deadline / Trigger` field may contain either a formal deadline
or the next external event that enables further work.

Examples:

- manuscript submission deadline,
- conference call-for-papers deadline,
- APC discount deadline,
- expected editorial feedback,
- camera-ready deadline,
- proceedings publication,
- DOI / bibliographic record becoming available.

Commercial deadlines such as APC discounts should not be confused with
formal submission deadlines.

## Bibliographic Completion

A publication is bibliographically complete when the final publication
can be unambiguously referenced.

Depending on the publication type, this may include:

- DOI,
- official proceedings entry,
- IEEE Xplore / SpringerLink / journal entry,
- volume, issue and page or article number,
- final bibliographic citation.

Use:

- `Pending` — bibliographic record is still incomplete,
- `Done` — final bibliographic record has been verified,
- `N/A` — no such bibliographic record is expected.

## Institutional Recognition

A publication is not necessarily administratively completed when it has
only been accepted, presented, or published.

Where applicable, the portfolio tracks the publication until:

1. the official bibliographic record is available,
2. the publication has been submitted for institutional recognition,
3. the corresponding recognition, reporting credit, or teaching-load
   reduction has been confirmed.

Use:

- `Pending` — institutional follow-up remains,
- `Submitted` — recognition has been requested,
- `Done` — recognition has been confirmed,
- `N/A` — no institutional recognition is intended.

This prevents completed publications from being overlooked for
institutional reporting or teaching-load reduction.
