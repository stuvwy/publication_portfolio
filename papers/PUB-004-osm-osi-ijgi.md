## Scientific Basis

The publication is based on the results of Anna Köberle's Master's thesis.

The work investigates the transformation of geospatial road-network
information into a representation suitable for autonomous-driving
simulation, with particular emphasis on the relationship between
OpenStreetMap (OSM), ASAM Open Simulation Interface (OSI), and the
ADSim simulation environment.

The journal publication should go beyond documenting the Master's thesis
and identify the scientific contribution and extensions required for an
IJGI publication.

## Strategic Context

The publication connects:
- geospatial information and digital road representations,
- OpenStreetMap,
- ASAM OSI,
- autonomous-driving simulation,
- ADSim,
- international collaboration with Shibaura Institute of Technology.
