The journal paper is therefore tracked separately and should develop the
conference work into an independent peer-reviewed scientific
publication.

## Scientific Scope

Current research direction includes:

- ASAM OSI as an interface for urban digital twins,
- reconstruction and resimulation of traffic situations,
- georeferencing and coordinate reference systems,
- deterministic simulation time,
- absolute scenario time,
- representation of environmental conditions,
- mapping urban infrastructure into simulation,
- reproducible scenario execution,
- optional OSI SensorView recording and replay.

The final scope should be defined based on the results available for the
ASAM International Conference presentation.
