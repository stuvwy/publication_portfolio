The publication opportunity originates from the integration of LISA+

## Scientific Scope

The potential publication could address the integration pipeline between
LISA+ and ADSim, including aspects such as:

- representation of traffic signal infrastructure,
- import and interpretation of LISA+ data,
- mapping into the ADSim simulation environment,
- relationship to ASAM OSI representations,
- reproducible reconstruction and resimulation of signalized
  intersections,
- limitations and interoperability aspects.

The final research question must be defined before committing to a
publication.

In particular, the contribution must be clearly distinguished from a
pure software-integration report.
