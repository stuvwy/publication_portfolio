## Motivation

The VASE project reaches its 20-year anniversary in 2026.

The anniversary provides an opportunity to revisit the original VASE
concept, results, and methodology from today's perspective and assess
their relevance for current automotive systems engineering, simulation,
and validation.

A visit to Validas on 16 November 2026 provides a concrete trigger for
discussing the publication concept and potential contributions.

## Scientific Basis

The publication should build on the original VASE project and its
publications and distinguish between:

1. the historical VASE objectives and results,
2. concepts that have remained relevant,
3. concepts that have evolved or been superseded,
4. lessons for current automotive development and validation,
5. potential connections to present-day simulation and ADSim research.

The paper should not be conceived merely as a retrospective anniversary
article. A scientific contribution and present-day research question
should be identified.
